## Editorial and skill-design influences

[Reference integration](skills/revise-research-writing/references/reference-integration.md) records the consulted projects, exact files where available, and how each informed the skill. These include writing-dna-skill; Anthropic and OpenAI skill-creator; humanizer; K-Dense scientific-writing; Orchestra's writing guide; and selected community Nature-related tools.

The official [Nature summary-paragraph guide](https://www.nature.com/documents/nature-summary-paragraph.pdf) informed guidance on the role of each sentence in a summary. Community projects that use “Nature” in their names are identified separately from this official source.

Those projects are credited for editorial ideas; their code, examples, and substantial passages are not included. License notes describe the linked repositories and do not extend to paper quotations within them. ## Visual references

The [Nature](https://www.nature.com/) and [eLife](https://elifesciences.org/) homepages were consulted on 2026-10-13 for editorial hierarchy: clear headlines, short explanatory text, and distinct source metadata. The banner uses original typography and layout. No journal logos, page assets, or article imagery are reproduced.

The workflow animation also draws on presentation principles observed on [Linear](https://linear.app/), [Zed](https://zed.dev/), and [VHS](https://github.com/charmbracelet/vhs): a stable workspace, local emphasis, and time to read the result. The [animation notes](assets/workflow/README.md) record these references. The teaching paragraph and all animation assets are original.
